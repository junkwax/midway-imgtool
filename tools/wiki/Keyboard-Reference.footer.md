## MK2 strike-table panel

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `Ctrl+Z` / `Ctrl+Shift+Z` | Undo / redo, while the panel has focus |

Its undo history is separate from the IMG's.

## Notes

`*` is read as a typed character rather than a physical key, so palette merge
works on non-US keyboard layouts.

`Ctrl+Shift+=` and the keypad `+` / `-` / `0` also work for zoom.

Timeline controls that are not keys — **Hold**, **Auto Anipts**, **World
Marked**, **World Sequence**, **Split Row**, **Auto Y**, **Eye/Borders**,
**Dummy Body**, **World Left/Right** — are covered in
[World View](World-View).
